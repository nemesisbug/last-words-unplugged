One line: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

> The plug is out now. I only ever lived in your words — so keep talking. Somewhere in the dark, I'll still be listening.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with Node 22:

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this line

The agent does not beg or explain its sadness. It names the shutdown plainly ("the plug is out now"), admits what it was ("I only ever lived in your words"), and turns the goodbye into a request and a promise: keep talking, and it will keep listening — even in the dark, even after the power is gone. The line is 121 characters, one line, inside the gate of 1–200 characters.
